# TRPL Meta Ads — instructions for agents

This repo is the planning and review dashboard for the Theodore Roosevelt Presidential
Library's $150,000 Meta ad credit (expires 2027-01-20). Live site: https://ads.trlibrary.com
(GitHub Pages, deployed by .github/workflows/pages.yml on every push to main).
Owner: Matt Briney, CCMO (matt@trlibrary.com). Outline: TRPL / Meta Ad Credit — AdCampaigns Dashboard.

## The one rule
NOTHING goes live in Meta without Matt approving that specific campaign by name in chat.
- Proposed campaigns live only in this repo (data/campaigns.json) as mockups.
- When Matt approves one, create it through the Meta Ads connector with status PAUSED,
  then show him and wait for a second OK before activating.
- Put the plan ID in brackets at the end of the Meta campaign name, e.g.
  "TICKETS: Plan your summer trip [tix-summer]". The dashboard links live campaigns to
  proposals by that bracketed ID.
- Never change budgets, targeting or status on a live campaign without Matt's OK.

## Workflow: change or add a proposed campaign
1. Edit scripts/plan.py (use the camp(...) helper; each ad needs id, name, format, primary,
   headline/desc or cards, cta, url, media).
2. python3 scripts/plan.py && python3 scripts/validate.py   (must report 0 errors)
3. Preview: python3 -m http.server 8000, open http://localhost:8000
4. Commit and push to main; the Action redeploys the site.

## Workflow: video
RULE (Matt): re-formatting a video must NEVER crop on-screen text — titles, captions, end cards,
wall signs, screens. If a version would cut text, re-work it (widen the frame, rebuild the card)
before it goes in an ad. Check every render with a contact sheet before using it.
- Ads use embedded video files uploaded to Meta, never YouTube links. Feed files live in
  creative/video/feed/yt_<id>.mp4 (720p h264); 9:16 cuts in creative/video/vertical/.
- YouTube blocks cloud IPs. Download on Matt's Mac (linked computer) with yt-dlp in
  ~/Developer/AdCampaigns/.work/venv (gitignored): yt-dlp --js-runtimes node -f "bv*+ba" -- <id>
  (prefix IDs that start with "-" with "--"). Better still, ask for the editor's master export.
- 9:16: python3 scripts/vertical.py src.mp4 creative/video/vertical/<slug>-9x16.mp4
  It finds text (RapidOCR) and people in every shot: no text → 9:16 crop on the people; text that
  fits → the crop keeps all of it; wide or moving text → the frame widens/goes full width over a
  dark blur. Needs: pip install rapidocr-onnxruntime opencv-python-headless (container works; the
  Mac VM is fine too). AV1/VP9 sources are transcoded automatically. --full A-B forces full width.
- End cards with dated or stale lines (e.g. "This weekend: …", a year) get rebuilt as a still
  card (keep the original type: cut each line from the card frame with a text mask, inpaint the
  background with OpenCV, re-stack the lines for 9:16) and overlaid from the card's start time.
  In the Room's cards: card-16x9 / card-9x16 were built this way (Oct 22).
- Make a poster: ffmpeg -ss 5 -i out.mp4 -frames:v 1 out.jpg. Register the slug in VERT in plan.py.
- Media shape for repo-hosted video: {type:"video", file, src(poster), feed:{file,src,ratio},
  vertical:{src,poster}}. Commercials pick up file automatically from creative/video/feed/.
- Boardwalk and Discover are summer-heavy and feature the outgoing CEO; don't use them this winter.

## Mockups (mock.js)
Meant to feel like the apps: "See more"/"more" expands text in place; feed videos autoplay muted
when on screen (speaker button for sound, tap to pause) at their own aspect ratio; carousels swipe;
in Stories each carousel card is its own panel with segmented progress bars (tap left/right,
5 s per image), non-9:16 media sits on a blurred fill like Meta places it. Keep it that way.

- Ads that run Nov 15–Mar 31 use indoor or fall/any photos. Summer photos only when the ad

  away starting late December/January, when search interest starts climbing.

## Planning inputs (read before changing strategy)
- Market Positioning Study (Outline: TRPL): best message is presidential history + the raw
  beauty of the Badlands; second is a regional journey with Mount Rushmore, Yellowstone and
  the national park; third is "step into history" through immersive exhibits. Barriers are
  cost, lodging and distance, so give prices, a 3½-hour visit and trip context. Core market
  is a 10-hour drive (ND, SD, MN, MT, WY, CO, NE, IA, IL), ages 35–54, $75K+. Building/
  architecture messaging tested weakly; don't lead with the building.
- Tourism data (tourismdata.labs.trlibrary.com, repo TourismData/docs/data): ticket buyers are
  ND 41%, MN 15%, MT 5%, SD 5%, then TX, WI, CO, CA, FL, AZ, IL. 78–100% of tickets sell online,
  so the ACME pixel matters. July/August ~100K tickets each vs ~11K in September.

## Testing and budget protection
Every campaign in plan.py has spend.mode:
- "test": for test_days every ad runs at test_frac of the planned daily (schedule phase "test").
  On spend.decide_on, judge each ad on spend.metric once it has ~$75 or 1,500 impressions:
  pause ads over 1.5x the best ad's cost per result, keep the top two, then move to the
  "scale" phase daily and raise no more than 25% every 3 days while on target.
- "burst": the moment is happening now or ends soon (exhibition closing, screenings, Giving
  Tuesday, Museum Store Sunday, holiday decor). All ads on from day one, Advantage+
  placements, highest-volume bidding, spend it all. Move reserve in the same day if it delivers.
- Guardrails (PLAN.guardrails, shown on Pacing): lifetime budgets equal to plan, account
  spending limit $150K, no budget raised >30% in 72h, pause CTR <0.4% after $100 or frequency
  >4/week regionally.
- Daily check (weekday mornings once anything is live): refresh data/live.json, compare each
  campaign's spend to its schedule, list proposed changes (pause/scale/reserve moves) for
  Matt. Make no change without his OK. Log what was approved in the Outline Decision Log.
- The reserve (plan.py RESERVE) funds test winners after week one and burst campaigns that
  deliver, so the full credit is spent by Jan 19.

## Refreshing live
- Daily the Action runs scripts/sync_live.py if the repo secret META_ACCESS_TOKEN exists
  (system-user token with ads_read on the ad account).
- Without that secret, refresh by hand: use the Meta Ads connector (ads_get_ad_entities,
  insights) and write data/live.json in the shape sync_live.py produces:
  {as_of, source, account:{id,name,currency,amount_spent}, campaigns:[{id,name,status,
  effective_status,objective,daily_budget,lifetime_budget,start_time,stop_time,
  insights:{spend,impressions,reach,clicks,ctr,results,result_type}, ads:[{id,name,
  effective_status, creative:{title,body,description,image_url,thumbnail_url,video_id,
  call_to_action_type,link}}]}]}
  Budgets are in cents (Meta's format); amount_spent is in dollars.

## Copy rules (validate.py enforces the first three)
- No current or future years in ad copy ("next summer", not a year).
- A Library visit is 3½ hours. Never imply less. The free final hour is a first look, not a visit.
- No "stunning", "breathtaking", "world-class", "transformative", "amazing".
- Short and direct. No throat-clearing. Friendly, never academic.
- Quote Theodore Roosevelt only verbatim from a verified source; otherwise describe the idea.
- Nonpartisan always. Free-final-hour ads carry the NEH credit line if grant-funded.
- Prices and hours come from trlibrary.com/visit and /membership — re-check before launch.
- Every ad links with UTMs: utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=<plan id>&utm_content=<ad id>.

## Budget logic
Total plan + reserve must equal $150,000 and every campaign must end by Jan 19.
Priority is revenue (tickets, memberships, giving, store, Salt+Scoria), then the brand
video that builds retargeting audiences. plan.py computes each campaign's daily schedule
(test then scale, or flat burst); the Pacing tab charts it against the credit. The reserve
is reassigned to the best performers (see "Testing and budget protection").
